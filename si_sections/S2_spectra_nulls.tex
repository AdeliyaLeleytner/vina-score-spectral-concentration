\sisection{S2. Spectral sensitivities and matched nulls}

\sisubsection{S2.1 Correlation and covariance spectra}

The frozen values are in \path{results/correlation_covariance_sensitivity.csv} and, with the
scalar records, in the \texttt{large\_vina\_panels} block of
\path{results/public_core_evidence.json}.

The correlation spectrum is primary because the question concerns target dependence after each
target column is placed on a common variance scale. The covariance spectrum keeps
target-specific variances instead. The result holds in direction under both scalings: removing
each ligand's mean across targets raises PR in both matrices under both
(Table~\ref{tab:s2spectra}). On the correlation surface PR rises from 1.834 to 9.303 for
Docking-44 and from 2.266 to 18.206 for DOCKSTRING-58. Through Equation~\ref{eq:s1pr} that is
the same statement as mean squared target correlation falling from 0.535 to 0.087 and from
0.431 to 0.038. On the covariance surface the rise is from 1.915 to 6.911 and from 2.474 to
8.235, so the direction is scaling-independent while the size is not.

The raw leading component is close to the uniform target direction in both matrices. Its
loading vector sits at cosine 0.9698 from that direction for Docking-44 and 0.9691 for
DOCKSTRING-58, with 43 of 44 and 57 of 58 loadings positive; the one negative loading in each
is $-0.0221$ and $-0.0122$. The PC1 scores correlate 0.9980 and 0.9973 with the ligand mean of
the column-standardised scores, so the leading component is close to an average of the panel.

Retaining the 5{,}393 positive DOCKSTRING scores instead of clipping them changes raw PR from
2.266 to 2.275 and residual PR from 18.206 to 18.362. Docking-44 holds no positive cell, so it
has no counterpart to this sensitivity.

\sisubsection{S2.2 Docking-44 missing scores}

The producer is \path{analysis/public_docking44_missing_data_sensitivity.py} and the frozen
outputs are \path{results/public_docking44_missing_data_sensitivity/summary.json} with the
four CSV files beside it.

Docking-44 carries 8{,}159 missing cells in 3{,}354 of its 12{,}651 rows. Two choices are
available: impute and keep every row, or delete incomplete rows and keep every observed score.
They give different answers (Table~\ref{tab:s2missing}). Target-mean imputation on the full
support gives residual PR 9.3029, target-median imputation 9.5279, and an additive fit on the
548{,}485 observed cells with each unobserved residual completed by its fitted value of zero
gives 9.6630. Complete-case deletion retains 9{,}297 rows and gives 13.9702, a rise of 4.6673
over the primary value.

The rise is not caused by imputation. Restricting the primary mean-imputed surface to exactly
the 9{,}297 complete rows reproduces the complete-case score matrix to a maximum absolute cell
difference of 0.0, so the two rows of Table~\ref{tab:s2missing} at 9{,}297 ligands are the same
matrix. What changed is which ligands are in the support.

To separate support from sample size we drew 1{,}000 random supports of exactly 9{,}297 rows
without replacement from the full primary surface, at seed 20260811. Their residual PR has
median 9.2940, mean 9.2951 and central 95\% repeated-support range 9.1281 to 9.4452. The
maximum over the 1{,}000 draws is below the complete-case value, so the fraction of random
supports at or below 13.9702 is 1.000. Reducing the row count from 12{,}651 to 9{,}297 does not
move residual PR; selecting these particular 9{,}297 rows moves it by 4.67.

Those rows are chemically distinct from the ones deletion removes. Complete rows have median
molecular weight 249.332~g/mol against 398.552 for excluded rows, a median shift of
149.220~g/mol at Cliff's $\delta=0.893$, and median heavy-atom count 17 against 28 at
$\delta=0.896$. Missingness is concentrated in one target: 4mqs holds 3{,}352 of the 8{,}159
missing cells. These are descriptive associations with row inclusion. Missingness was not
randomised, so the equal-$n$ control is a support-selection diagnostic and not a population
test, and the zero-residual completion is an additive-model sensitivity rather than recovery of
unknown docking scores.

\sisubsection{S2.3 The four transformation-matched null constructions}

The producer is \path{analysis/public_residual_null_audit.py} and every frozen output sits in
\path{results/public_residual_null_audit/}. Distribution summaries, seeds and support digests
are in \path{summary.json} there, each size-conditioned replicate is in
\path{mw_conditional_null_repeats.csv}, and each bin edge is in
\path{mw_conditional_bin_contract.csv}.

\paragraph{The common contract.} Within each matrix all four families run on one deterministic
simple-random support of 12{,}000 ligands drawn without replacement, at seed 60 for Docking-44
(from 12{,}651 rows) and seed 61 for DOCKSTRING-58 (from 260{,}060 rows). Each support is
recorded as the SHA-256 of its sorted zero-based row indices encoded as little-endian int64:
\texttt{8253cb55c373} for Docking-44 and \texttt{64dccaf8b65d} for DOCKSTRING-58. Each family
runs 500 repetitions, and every simulated surface passes through the same centring, scaling and
spectral steps as the observed matrix, so only the generating mechanism differs. On these
supports the observed residual PR is 9.295 for Docking-44 and 18.158 for DOCKSTRING-58, against
$P-1$ bounds of 43 and 57.

\paragraph{Additive Gaussian.} We fit a grand mean with ligand and target effects, then add
independent Gaussian noise whose standard deviation is the observed residual standard deviation
of each target column. This preserves both main-effect structures and the target-wise noise
scale, and destroys any dependence between target columns beyond those effects. It also gives a
check on the raw spectrum: the null median raw PR is 1.891 against an observed 1.837 for
Docking-44, and 2.283 against 2.212 for DOCKSTRING-58, so the construction reproduces the raw
map closely while missing the residual one.

\paragraph{Empirical residual-column permutation.} We permute each observed residual target
column independently across ligands, then repeat centring and target scaling. The permutation
step preserves the empirical marginal distribution of every target residual exactly. It
destroys cross-target residual correspondence and each ligand's residual scale.

\paragraph{Row-norm-preserving random direction.} We replace each ligand's residual by an
independent random direction in the target-zero-sum subspace, rescaled to that ligand's
observed residual Euclidean norm. This preserves the ligand support, the target count, the zero
row sums and every residual row norm exactly. It destroys target residual marginals, target
variances and cross-target alignment.

\paragraph{Size-conditioned permutation.} We permute each processed raw target column
independently within deterministic molecular-weight bins, then row-centre and target-scale.
The permutation keeps each target's exact empirical raw-score marginal inside each bin, so
bin-specific location and scale survive, and so does the non-Gaussian shape of the score
distribution. It destroys ligand correspondence across targets and any continuous
molecular-weight trend inside a bin, and row centring afterwards changes target marginals. Ten
equally populated bins are the primary design; 1, 5 and 20 bins are sensitivities, and a
size-matched random partition with identical bin sizes is the control that holds bin sizes
while carrying no size information.

\paragraph{The ten-bin contract.} Molecular weight is \texttt{Descriptors.MolWt} from RDKit
2025.03.6 on the released row SMILES \cite{rdkit2025}. Quantile edges are deduplicated and
\texttt{np.searchsorted(side='left')} assigns every exactly tied value to one bin, which is why
bin sizes vary between 1{,}197 and 1{,}203 rather than sitting at 1{,}200
(Table~\ref{tab:s2bins}). The bin-label vector is itself recorded as a SHA-256 of the
support-order labels encoded as little-endian int16: \texttt{0c5b40e02258} for Docking-44 and
\texttt{0d6f84fc2e99} for DOCKSTRING-58.

\paragraph{Results.} Table~\ref{tab:s2nulls} gives every null median with its central 95\%
simulation range. In all eight dataset-by-family combinations, each of the 500 simulated
residual PR values exceeds the observed value, so the empirical lower-tail probability is
$(0+1)/(500+1)=0.0020$ throughout, which is the resolution floor of a 500-repetition design.
The three families that ignore molecular size sit within 0.60 of the $P-1$ bound. The
size-conditioned null is the only one that moves away from it, to 0.811 and 0.825 of the bound.
What each family removes is stated above. None of them establishes docking accuracy or
biological correctness, and none reaches past these two fixed Vina panels.

\paragraph{How much coarse-size conditioning reproduces.} We measure the size-conditioned null against its own
one-bin version, which is the same construction with no size information at all. The one-bin
null medians are 42.843 and 56.728. Ten-bin conditioning closes 23.8\% of the distance from
that one-bin median to the observed value for Docking-44 and 25.1\% for DOCKSTRING-58. Finer
binning adds little: the closed share is 19.1\%, 23.8\% and 25.7\% at 5, 10 and 20 bins for
Docking-44, and 22.8\%, 25.1\% and 26.0\% for DOCKSTRING-58. A size-matched random partition
with the same ten bin sizes but no size information stays at median PR 42.844 and 56.726 and
closes nothing, at $-4.2\times10^{-6}$ and $2.8\times10^{-5}$. The shift is therefore reproduced
by molecular-weight-aligned bins rather than by an equally sized random partition.

\paragraph{What the same null does reproduce.} Coarse-size conditioning closes 23.8\% and 25.1\% of
the excess concentration, and it still reproduces much of the edge ordering. The ten-bin null map
agrees with the observed residual map at median Spearman 0.924, with 95\% simulation range
0.921 to 0.928, for Docking-44, and 0.718 with range 0.707 to 0.728 for DOCKSTRING-58; median
edge-sign agreement is 0.834 and 0.727. The size-matched random partition reaches 0.051 and
0.052 Spearman with sign agreement 0.517 and 0.514, so the ordering is recovered specifically by
molecular-weight-aligned conditioning in this design. This does not identify molecular weight as
the operative cause. Its overlap with the strongest observed edges is much
weaker: the median Jaccard index against the 25 largest-magnitude observed edges is 0.087 for
Docking-44 and 0.351 for DOCKSTRING-58. A null can therefore rank the edges of the residual map
without concentrating it.
