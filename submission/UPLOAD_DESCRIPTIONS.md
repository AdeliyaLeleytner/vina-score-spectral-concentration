## Graphical abstract

**Title:** Removing a shared ligand-wide axis reveals a library-dependent target map

**Description and alt text:** A docking-score matrix contains a strong ligand-wide gradient.
Subtracting each ligand's mean reveals a residual target-correlation map that is more structured
than matched nulls. A 200-ligand pilot drawn from the source library closely recovers the full map.

**Description and alt text:** Scatter plot comparing pocket volume with the target-specific
molecular-weight slope that remains after removal of the ligand-wide score offset. The association
